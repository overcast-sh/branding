# Voice & writing

Overcast talks like a good CLI: precise, calm, a little dry. It never
oversells — the product's whole promise is *less* drama than the real cloud.

## Principles

- **Plain over clever.** "Runs AWS APIs on localhost" beats "reimagines cloud development".
- **Numbers with receipts.** Every performance claim states what was measured and how.
- **Honest about limits.** A `501` with an explanation beats a divergent `200` — in the product and in the copy.
- **Lowercase wordmark, sentence-case everything else.** Headings aren't Title Case.

## Third-party marks

Overcast names AWS and its services only to state compatibility, and only in
plain text. That is the line AWS draws itself: nominative use "should be in plain
text only (no logos)" ([AWS Trademark Guidelines](https://aws.amazon.com/trademark-guidelines/) §13),
and imitating AWS trade dress, including its "product icons", is prohibited (§10).
The [AWS Architecture Icons](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons/) ship with
no licence and are offered for diagrams, not product UIs. So: service icons are
lucide or original glyphs on Overcast's own categorical ramp — no AWS icons or
logos on any surface, not even behind an opt-in, and no lookalikes.
